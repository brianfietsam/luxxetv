“Copy m3u link” in the player or a program’s menu gives a link to one channel that opens in VLC, TiviMate or any IPTV app, without your web password. Your provider login is never in the link: it plays through this PC, so each viewer uses one of your provider connections. Without a web password, links only work on your home network.
